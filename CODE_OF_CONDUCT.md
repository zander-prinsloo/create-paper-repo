# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include
respectful disagreement, welcoming questions, and focusing feedback on the
work rather than the person. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment,
personal attacks, discrimination, and publishing private information without
permission.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact details
on their GitHub profile. Reports will be reviewed and handled in confidence.
